# 🦇 Venator (VR)

Venator is a short VR horror experience (10+ minutes) developed by a team of six, focused on delivering a tense, narrative-driven journey where immersion and atmosphere take center stage.

Set in a confined and unsettling castle, the game emphasizes scale, limited visibility, and sound design to create constant pressure. Players must navigate through a linear experience that blends exploration and combat while maintaining a strong sense of vulnerability and tension.

---

## 🧠 Gameplay

Venator is designed as a linear, story-driven experience where pacing and immersion are key.

![Gameplay](GIF1.gif)

Players explore a dark and oppressive environment using intuitive VR interactions, managing limited resources and reacting to threats as they arise. The gameplay loop revolves around:

- **Exploration** → Carefully navigating spaces with restricted visibility
- **Tension Management** → Using sound and environmental cues to anticipate danger
- **Combat & Survival** → Reacting quickly with a streamlined inventory system
- **Immersion** → Experiencing the world through spatial audio, lighting, and scale

![Inventory](GIF2.gif)

A quick-switch inventory system allows players to seamlessly transition between exploration and combat, maintaining flow and avoiding disruption in high-pressure moments.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Download the game from the [Releases](https://github.com/Venator-XR/Venator-VR/releases/) section.  
   *Two versions are available: Standalone and PCVR.*

2. **PCVR:**  
   Unzip the downloaded file and run `Venator.exe`.

3. **Standalone** *(tested on Meta Quest 3)*:  
   Unzip the file and install it on your headset as a new app.  
   *If you're unsure how to do this, you can follow any sideloading guide for Meta Quest.*
